THE BASICS
Make yourself at home.
W A S D Move
Mouse Look around
Space Jump / swim up
Left Shift Sneak / fly down
Left Ctrl Sprint
E Inventory & crafting
Left click Hold to mine / attack
Right click Place / use / eat
1 – 9 / Wheel Select hotbar
Q Drop one item
F3 Debug coordinates
Esc Pause / close inventory
Gather wood. Arrange ingredients in the 2×2 grid, or click a recipe to fill it. Take the result. Use a crafting table for 3×3 recipes and armor. Stone needs a pickaxe. Eat apples or cooked food to restore hunger. Build shelter before nightfall. Furnaces need input and fuel; coal smelts ten items. Hoe grass or dirt, plant seeds, and grow wheat near water for bread. Mining grass blocks can drop seeds. Right-click armor to equip it. Creepers flash before exploding—keep your distance! Right-click a table, furnace, chest, or bed to use it.
Creative: unlimited blocks, no damage, double-tap Space to fly. Middle-click picks a block. Inventory: click to move stacks, right-click to split or place one, Shift-click to move between inventory and hotbar.
